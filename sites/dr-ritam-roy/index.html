    About the doctor

    Evidence-based medicine,
    delivered with patience.

    Dr. Ritam Roy is a consultant physician and diabetologist serving the Kanchrapara and Kalyani region near Kolkata. He trained at the Institute of Post Graduate Medical Education & Research (IPGMER) and SSKM Hospital, and went on to specialise in diabetes and endocrinology through the Royal College of Physicians (UK) and the Public Health Foundation of India.

    Beyond the chamber, he is known for his social work — organising free health camps, diabetes awareness drives and medical support for underprivileged families.

          Good to know

          Frequently asked

          Should I come fasting?

          If you have been advised fasting blood tests, come after 8–10 hours of fasting (water is fine) and get the sample taken before breakfast. Otherwise, eat normally and take your usual medicines unless told otherwise.

          What should I bring?

          All previous prescriptions, recent blood reports (sugar, HbA1c, thyroid, kidney function, lipid profile), your glucometer log and the medicines or insulin you currently use.

          How does the serial system work?

          Your booking request reserves a place in the queue for that session. The clinic confirms your serial number on WhatsApp. Patients are seen in serial order; emergencies may be seen first.

          Can I get my reports reviewed without a full visit?

          Choose “Report review” as the visit type when booking. Dr. Roy will advise whether a full consultation is needed.

          What if my sugar is very high or very low right now?

          Sugar below 70 mg/dL with symptoms: take 15 g of glucose (3 teaspoons of sugar in water) and recheck in 15 minutes. Sugar above 300 mg/dL with vomiting, drowsiness or breathlessness is an emergency — go to the nearest hospital.
